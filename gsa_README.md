## Sales Mastery Framework

Five stages of selling. See the full [sales framework](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework).

| Stage | What it covers | Programmes |
|---|---|---|
| [Attract](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework#attract) | Visibility, credibility, prospecting, social selling | Personal Branding for Sales Professionals, LinkedIn & Social Selling, Prospecting in the Age of AI |
| [Diagnose](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework#diagnose) | Understand the customer and ask better questions | Consultative Solution Selling, The Discovery Advantage |
| [Influence](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework#influence) | Turn insight into value, stories and recommendations | Value to Win, StorySelling, Pitch to Win, Influence to Win |
| [Commit](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework#commit) | Handle objections, negotiate, move to next steps | The Objection Advantage, Powerful Negotiations, Opportunity Mastery |
| [Grow](https://www.theglobalsalesacademy.com/our-sales-framework#grow) | Deepen trust and grow accounts | Growth to Win, Strategic Account Management, Service to Sales |
