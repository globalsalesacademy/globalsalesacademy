Our sales framework

What is the Sales Mastery Framework™?

The Sales Mastery Framework™ is GSA's five-stage approach to selling: Attract, Diagnose, Influence, Commit and Grow. Each stage links to the programmes that build that skill. See the full our sales framework page.

StageWhat it coversProgrammes
1. AttractCreate opportunity. Build visibility, credibility and relevance through your personal brand, prospecting, social selling and networking.Personal Branding for Sales Professionals™, LinkedIn & Social Selling™, Prospecting in the Age of AI™
2. DiagnoseUnderstand the customer. Research the account, ask better questions and listen closely to uncover what really matters.Consultative Solution Selling™, The Discovery Advantage™
3. InfluenceCreate value. Turn insight into persuasive value, stories and recommendations, and present them in a way that moves people to act.Value to Win™, StorySelling™, Pitch to Win™, Influence to Win™
4. CommitCreate action. Handle objections, negotiate on value and move decisions forward to clear next steps.The Objection Advantage™, Powerful Negotiations™, Opportunity Mastery™
5. GrowExpand the relationship. Deepen trust and create long-term customer value through account growth, cross-selling and retention.Growth to Win™, Strategic Account Management™, Service to Sales™

How it works

How does customised enablement work?

The process follows five steps, from understanding your business to scaling the methodology.

1. Understand

Learn about your business, people, customers and sales priorities.

2. Build

Select, adapt and develop programmes, methodology, tools and playbooks.

3. Activate

Bring it to life through learning, practice, coaching and application.

4. Embed

Reinforce with playbooks, manager coaching and certification.

5. Scale

Support Train-the-Trainer and licensing so the methodology can grow.

Every sales organisation is different. Share how your team sells, and GSA will suggest where to start.
